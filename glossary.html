Appendix A — Glossary · Glosario

This glossary is a teaching resource, designed for specialised-translation practice: plain-language definitions, Spanish equivalents and notes on common pitfalls. It is not a validated terminological corpus, and its entries are not linked to documentary sources. For authoritative usage, consult the diagnostic classifications and the clinical guidance cited in the chapters.

A.1 Core terms

NoteTwo Spanish names for ARFID

Spanish-language sources do not agree on a single name for ARFID. Two forms in use are trastorno de evitación/restricción de la ingesta de alimentos (with the acronym TERIA) and trastorno por evitación/restricción… (keeping the English acronym ARFID). A translator should adopt one, state its source (the Spanish version of ICD-11 or the Spanish edition of DSM-5-TR) and use it consistently throughout a text.
